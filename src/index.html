PRIVACY & SECURITY

VYRON loads maintained advertising and tracking filter lists and caches them locally. If the lists cannot be downloaded, it falls back to a smaller built-in domain list. No filter engine catches every ad or tracking method. Site camera, microphone, location, notifications, and other permission requests are denied by default.
